### S1 · 0:00–1:03 · 五个运营问题

**时长 / 词数 / 语速**：63s · 132 词 · 126 wpm · 功能：五个运营问题

- **页面 / 路由**：`03-web-page/rm-workbench.html?record=1#/overview`

- **屏幕动作**：不点击。按新叙事顺序 IPO → onboarding → estate → revenue → failure 给对应卡加红色描边高亮（01 → 03 → 02 → 04 → 05）；最后停在右上角 “Public cases + demo data” 状态条。
- **屏幕标注**：左下常驻合规角标；第 10 秒浮出一行：Five operating problems — not five product slides。
- **人物内心（导演层，不占口播）**：COO：我们买了系统，却仍然回答不了五个最基本的问题。
- **转场**：硬切到 J1–J10 静态卡；保持同一音乐床。

**英文口播（逐字）**

> Five operating problems. First, how CIB originates an IPO and how Private Banking onboards founders and executives on a consent basis. Second, how a new client, a next-generation member or a spouse completes a first investment. Third, where the family’s money actually goes twelve months after a death. Fourth, how the Private Bank is actually performing — AUM, revenue, client commission, gross margin, net margin and cost-to-income, with the formulas on one dashboard. Fifth, why a trade fails — client, market, RM, operations or IT — and how the RM turns that attribution into knowledge and a better client conversation. This workbench is not a marketing site. It decomposes each problem into business steps, fields, status, ownership and evidence. The public names are structural references only, not HSBC clients. All bank figures are demo data.

**中文对照**

> 五个真实的经营问题。第一，CIB 做 IPO，PB 如何合规承接创始人和高管；第二，新客户、二代和配偶如何完成第一次投资；第三，一个客户身故一年以后，家族的钱到底走到了哪里；第四，私人银行到底经营得怎么样——AUM、收入、客户佣金、毛利、净利、成本收入比，以及这些数字背后的公式，能不能放在一张 dashboard 上；第五，一笔交易为什么失败——是客户、市场、RM、运营还是 IT 的原因——以及 RM 如何把失败归因沉淀成知识和下一次更好的客户沟通。这个工作台不是营销页面，而是把每个问题的业务步骤、字段、状态、责任人和证据拆开。所有公开人物只是结构参考，不表示他们是汇丰客户；所有银行数字都是演示数据。

**时长 / 词数 / 语速**：47s · 102 词 · 130 wpm · 功能：估值治理

- **页面 / 路由**：`03-web-page/rm-workbench.html?record=1#/ipo-valuation`
- **画面**：估值页：三种方法（公开轮 / 可比 / DCF）与权重、折扣表、区间、LTV 指引、委员会证据清单。
- **运镜**：自上而下 2 秒 scroll；1.2 推进到折扣表；说到区间时框选 US$26.7–29.0bn；最后下摇到 committee approver。
- **屏幕动作**：hover 三种方法；停在折扣表三列（marketability 30 / minority 10 / concentration 10）；highlight “control premium excluded”；滚动到 committee。
- **屏幕标注**：屏幕浮层：no committee approval → no lendable value；右侧：valuation date + evidence。
- **人物内心（导演层，不占口播）**：客户：这个估值能解释吗？银行凭什么拿它给我贷款？

**英文口播（逐字）**

> This is pre-IPO valuation; a valuation is not a price target, it is an explainable range for credit, pledge, trust and client communication. For DeepSeek, the July 2026 reported post-money — roughly US$49–56bn — is the primary input, with comparable companies and DCF alongside; each method carries weight, inputs and a range. The same stack — marketability 30, minority 10, concentration 10 — is applied to both ends, giving US$26.7–29.0bn, and a 10–30% LTV guide turns it into indicative lendable value.
